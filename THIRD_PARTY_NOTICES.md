# Third-party notices

Dorangori uses AndroidX, Jetpack Compose, Wear Compose, Google Play services (Wearable Data Layer), Kotlin and kotlinx libraries (Apache-2.0). Speech recognition runs in OndolSaem (see its notices), reached through the OndolSaem SDK.

The Wear app constrains AndroidX Fragment to 1.8.9 (Apache-2.0) for compatibility with ActivityResult permission APIs.
